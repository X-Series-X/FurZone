Helpful Tips

Start smaller if budget is tight

A good head or mini-partial gives a lot of character presence for much less than a fullsuit.

Check portfolios carefully

Look at multiple finished works, not just one nice photo. Ask about materials, ventilation, and vision.

Payment plans are common

Most fursuit makers offer deposits + monthly payments. Never pay 100% upfront to unknown sellers.

Therian masks vs fursuit heads

Therian masks are usually simpler, lighter, and much cheaper. Full fursuit heads are more durable and detailed but cost significantly more.

Watch for red flags

No portfolio, pressure to pay quickly, prices that seem too good to be true, or poor communication are warning signs.

Prices rise with complexity

Extra colors, markings, moving jaws, special eyes, wings, or digitigrade padding all increase the final cost.
